There are several ways to correct this problem. You can decrease the condenser chilling water temperature, increase the flow, or do both. Or you can decrease the bath temperature , but in that case , you must also decrease the vacuum . Release the vacuum when distillation is complete. 0 Separate the oil from the solvent.
